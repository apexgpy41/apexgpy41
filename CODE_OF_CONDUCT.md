# 🌍 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or skill level.

By participating in this project, you agree to help create a positive community.

---

# 🤝 Expected Behavior

Please:

- Be respectful.
- Be professional.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Help other contributors.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.

---

# 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Hate speech
- Trolling
- Spam
- Publishing private information
- Any abusive or threatening behavior

---

# 🛡 Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers will:

- Review issues fairly.
- Respond respectfully.
- Help contributors.
- Remove inappropriate content.
- Take action against abusive behavior.

---

# ⚖ Enforcement

Depending on the severity, maintainers may:

- Issue a warning
- Remove comments or content
- Temporarily restrict participation
- Permanently ban repeat offenders

---

# ❤️ Our Goal

Our goal is to build a professional, friendly, and collaborative open-source community where everyone can learn and contribute safely.

Thank you for helping make this project better.
